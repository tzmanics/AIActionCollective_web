# AIAC Content List

-----

## TOPIC LIST WITH SUBTOPICS

### 1. What is AI actually doing to jobs right now

- The jobs already being automated and the ones next in line
- The difference between AI replacing jobs vs. augmenting them
- What “AI-assisted” actually means on a daily basis for real workers

### 2. What Peter Frase’s Four Futures means and which one we’re headed toward

- Breaking down the two axes: abundance vs. scarcity, equality vs. hierarchy
- Which future are we currently building without realizing it
- What it would actually take politically to land in the good quadrant

### 3. The replicator analogy: what Star Trek gets right about AI and abundance

- What the replicator actually represents as a technology metaphor
- The real-world tech we’re building that maps to it right now
- Why the Federation didn’t happen automatically and what that means for us

### 4. Who owns AI and why that matters

- How a handful of companies control the most powerful AI systems
- What it means that AI was trained on all of our collective data and labor
- What public or cooperative ownership of AI could look like

### 5. What an AI productivity tax is and how it could work

- What productivity gains actually are and who is capturing them right now
- How a robot tax or AI dividend could work in practice
- Real examples of places already experimenting with this thinking

### 6. How AI is already shortening workweeks in some places

- Companies that have moved to 4-day workweeks using AI and what happened
- What the research says about productivity and worker wellbeing
- What would need to change politically for this to become the norm

### 7. How a progressive organizer can use AI today

- Writing canvassing scripts, donor emails, and social copy faster
- Using AI for opposition research and policy analysis on a small budget
- Automating repetitive tasks so organizers can spend time with people

### 8. How a candidate can use AI on a small budget

- Free and low-cost AI tools any campaign can use starting today
- Using AI to understand your district, your voters, and your opponent
- How small campaigns can punch above their weight with AI-assisted communications

### 9. What “AI is built on collective human knowledge” actually means

- How large language models are trained and where that data comes from
- Why that makes AI a collective resource, not a private invention
- What we could demand in return as the people whose knowledge built it
